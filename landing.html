Frequently Asked

Is AabSara really free, or is it a trial?+
Genuinely free, forever, under the MIT License. There is no trial period, no feature gate and no account. The project is community-funded by people who want a recorder without a subscription.
How does it beat Action! and Bandicam?+
Those apps charge $37–$49 a year for a subset of what AabSara ships. AabSara adds green-screen compositing, multi-destination streaming and built-in stream overlays — features that only appear in the $299 Camtasia tier — and runs on Linux and macOS too.
Will recording hurt my game performance?+
No. Capture and encoding happen on the GPU through NVENC, AMD AMF or Intel QuickSync, so the CPU stays nearly idle and your frame rate barely moves. The Rust/Tauri core is designed around a low memory footprint.
Can I record games in 4K at 120 FPS?+
Yes. AabSara supports 4K at up to 120 frames per second with full hardware encoding. Your refresh rate, GPU encoder and disk speed are the real limits.
Where can I see the source code?+
On GitHub at github.com/CyberPrince-glitch/AabSara. The full Rust Tauri backend, React + TypeScript frontend and build scripts are public and open to modification and contribution.
